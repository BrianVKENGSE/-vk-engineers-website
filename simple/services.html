Capabilities

A full-service practice.

VK Engineers provides integrated structural engineering, architecture, civil engineering, construction administration, and permitting services for residential, commercial, and multi-family projects throughout California.

Services · General

Project delivery, start to finish.

These services support every project regardless of discipline.

Construction Administration

Site observations, contractor coordination, RFI responses, shop drawing and submittal reviews, clarification of design intent, and field coordination through completion.

Permit Processing

Permit coordination and processing from initial submission through agency review and approval — navigating jurisdictional requirements, plan-check comments, and resubmittals.

Plan Review & Peer Review

Independent structural plan reviews and third-party peer checks for developers, municipalities, architects, and engineers.

Value Engineering

Practical solutions that optimize structural systems, materials, construction methods, and project costs without compromising safety or design intent.

Project Coordination

Integrated coordination between owners, architects, engineers, contractors, consultants, and permitting agencies.

Discipline · 01

Architecture

Architecture

Full-service architectural design from concept and space planning through construction documents and permitting. We develop practical, contextual designs for custom homes, multi-family developments, commercial buildings, additions, and renovations.

Discipline · 02

Structural Engineering

Structural Engineering

Complete structural engineering for new construction, additions, remodels, multi-family, commercial, and mixed-use developments — steel, reinforced concrete, wood, and masonry systems designed for California's seismic conditions.

Concrete Building Division

Specialized concrete design and engineering: reinforced concrete systems, foundations, slabs, retaining structures, and other cast-in-place applications.

Building Assessments & Existing Conditions

Comprehensive structural and building assessments, including site investigations, structural evaluations, damage assessments, and engineering reports.

Seismic Retrofits

Engineering solutions for existing buildings requiring seismic upgrades — assessment through construction administration.

Soft-Story Retrofits

Ordinance-compliant seismic upgrades for soft-story buildings, from assessment through city submittals and construction administration.

Structural Investigations & Reports

Existing-condition assessments, damage evaluations, and stamped engineering letters and reports.

Discipline · 03

Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering

Site planning, grading and drainage, utility coordination, stormwater considerations, and civil permitting for residential and commercial land development. Delivered in-house or coordinated with qualified specialist partners when required.

Discipline · 04

Specialty Structural Engineering

Specialty Structural Engineering

Specialized engineering for unique and technically demanding applications, including vibration control, mass timber, connection engineering, overhead crane girders, inertia mass foundations, conveyance supports, equipment supports, and robotic system supports.
