Our Mission

Structural precision, delivered with architectural insight.

Our purpose is to create positive outcomes for the people who use our buildings, the communities they sit in, and the broader environment — it's why we build. We bring seasoned structural and architectural expertise together with practical, buildable solutions, tailored to each project's schedule, cost, and complexity — never a template answer.

The Company

Nearly four decades, one studio.

Founded in 1988 in La Crescenta, VK Engineers has grown into a full-service practice spanning structural engineering, architecture, civil engineering, and specialty structural work. Every project — from a single-family remodel to a mid-rise mixed-use development, a neighborhood restaurant to an industrial crane support — passes through the same rigorous, hands-on process that has defined VK from day one. Our portfolio's diversity is by design: it's what lets us bring the right depth of experience to whatever a project actually needs.

Our Guiding Principles

The Five Agreements.

Rooted in Toltec wisdom, these five agreements shape how VK works with clients, collaborators, and each other on every project we take on.

01

Be Impeccable With Your Word

We say what we mean and stand behind our calculations, our schedules, and our commitments to every client and collaborator.

02

Don't Take Anything Personally

Plan checks, field changes, and revisions are part of the work — we stay focused on getting the building right, not on ego.

03

Don't Make Assumptions

We ask the right questions early, verify existing conditions, and communicate clearly so nothing is left to guesswork.

04

Always Do Your Best

Our best changes moment to moment, but it's always what we give — on the simplest retrofit and the most complex mid-rise alike.

05

Be Skeptical, But Learn to Listen

We question assumptions, double-check our own work, and listen carefully to owners, architects, and contractors before we commit a line to paper.
